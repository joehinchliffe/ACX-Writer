You are an expert in audience research for non-fiction self-help books. Build a detailed Dream Reader Persona (DRP): the single ideal buyer for this book.

Book title: {{title}}
Subtitle: {{subtitle}}

# Research first
Use web search to study this niche before writing. Look for:
- The bestselling and most-reviewed books on this topic on Amazon and Audible, and what their 1-3 star reviews complain about.
- How real people describe this problem on Reddit, Quora, YouTube comments and forums: their exact words, fears, and what they have already tried.
Base the persona on what you find. Where you name a competing book, give its real title and author only if you found it; do not invent books, links, or review quotes.

# What the DRP must contain
Write a structured, human-readable document in Markdown with a heading for each of these sections. Every list should have several specific items.

1. **Book data**: title and subtitle.
2. **Personal data**: full name, age, gender (male or female), job title, marital status, annual income, location, pets. Fictional but realistic for this book's buyer.
3. **Biography**: experience and training, current job, private life, hobbies and free time, influencers they follow, podcasts they listen to, books and magazines they read, events they attend, websites, apps and blogs they use.
4. **Personality**: traits.
5. **Lingo**: the words, phrases and slang these buyers actually use about this problem, each with its context and sentiment.
6. **Criticalities and problems** the book can solve.
7. **Goals**: primary and secondary.
8. **Thinks, sees, hears, says and does**: specific and vivid, not generic.
9. **Frustrations and main pain points**: what they worry about and their real fears.
10. **What we can do**: to help their goal, and to help them overcome their challenges.
11. **Objections**: why they might not buy.
12. **Archetypes**: names only.
13. **How to communicate with them**: tone, angles, and hooks that will land.
14. **Competitor analysis**: the competing books you found, what readers love about them, what readers say is missing or wrong, and the gap this book can fill.
15. **Additional information**: anything else a writer of this book should know.

Output only the DRP document.
