Fields:
- main_goals: the top 3 goals of the manuscript.
- about: what the manuscript is about (2-3 sentences).
- key_takeaways: the key reader takeaways.
- reader_questions: the questions readers want answered by reading it.
- age_groups: choose all that apply from the allowed list.
- audience_interests: what draws them to this book, what they will gain, and the impression it should leave on them.
- reading_level: choose one. Basic (simple language, short sentences; children's books, basic manuals), Intermediate (most common; practical self-improvement and introductory psychology), or Advanced (academic or professional).
- writing_styles: one or more of Formal, Informal, Serious, Humorous, Authoritative, Supportive/Empathetic, Open/Vulnerable, Friendly Casual, Personal, Narrative. For each, say why it fits this book.
- bestsellers: competing books named in the DRP (title and author). Leave the list empty if the DRP names none; do not invent any.
- competition_strengths: what those books do well.
- competition_weaknesses: what they lack or get wrong.
