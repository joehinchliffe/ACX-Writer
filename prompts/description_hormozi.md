# Style
- Short. Punchy. One idea per sentence. Each sentence opens a small curiosity gap that the next one closes while opening a bigger one.
- Direct, conversational, almost confrontational: a trusted friend who refuses to let you make excuses. No hedging, no passive voice, no throat-clearing.
- Agitate the pain hard before offering relief. Make the cost of doing nothing feel immediate. Use pattern interrupts. Bold claims, backed at once by specifics.
- Cut anything that slows the reader down.

# Structure
- Hook (1-2 sentences): impossible to ignore.
- Pain (3-4 short sentences): make it real and make it sting.
- Risk (2-3 sentences): what happens if they don't act.
- Solution (2-3 sentences): the book as the answer, stated with confidence.
- Bullets (5 or 7): punchy benefit, bold opener, specific payoff. Strongest first, second-strongest last.
- Close (2-3 sentences): the transformation in one vivid sentence, then a direct call to action.

# Rules
- Amazon policy: no guaranteed results, success rates, cures, or unverifiable statistics ("proven", "guaranteed", "100%", "no-fail"). Confident, never false.
- Under 4,000 characters. Second person. Paragraphs of 1-3 sentences.
